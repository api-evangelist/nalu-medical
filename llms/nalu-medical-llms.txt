# Nalu Medical

> Nalu Medical, Inc. is a privately held, commercial-stage medical device company in Carlsbad,
> California. It develops the Nalu Neurostimulation System: a battery-free micro implantable pulse
> generator (mIPG) powered wirelessly by an externally worn Therapy Disc and controlled by the
> patient through a smartphone remote-control app, FDA-cleared for spinal cord stimulation (SCS) and
> peripheral nerve stimulation (PNS) for chronic pain.
